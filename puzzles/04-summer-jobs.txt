title: Summer Jobs
difficulty: Medium
story: Four teenagers each started a different summer job on a different day this week, and each earns a different hourly wage. Match each teen with their job, start day and pay.
Teens: Lena, Marco, Nia, Omar
Jobs: lifeguard, cashier, tutor, dog walker
Start days: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday
Pay: $10, $12, $14, $16
clue: The tutor earns the most.
clue: Marco started the day before Omar.
clue: The dog walker started last and earns the least.
clue: Neither Nia nor Marco is the tutor.
clue: Omar earns less than Marco.
clue: The cashier earns $2 less than the lifeguard.
clue: Lena started on Wednesday.
answer: Lena, tutor, Wednesday, $16
answer: Marco, lifeguard, Monday, $14
answer: Nia, dog walker, Thursday, $10
answer: Omar, cashier, Tuesday, $12
