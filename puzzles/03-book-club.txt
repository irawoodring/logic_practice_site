title: Book Club
difficulty: Medium
story: Four members of a book club each started a different kind of book this week, read a different number of pages, and brought a different snack to the meeting. Match each member with their book, pages and snack.
Members: Hana, Ivan, June, Kofi
Books: mystery, biography, sci-fi, poetry
Pages: 50, 75, 100, 125
Snacks: apples, cookies, grapes, popcorn
clue: The poetry reader read 25 fewer pages than the biography reader.
clue: June read more pages than Hana.
clue: Whoever brought grapes is reading the mystery.
clue: Kofi brought popcorn.
clue: Ivan read the fewest pages.
clue: The sci-fi reader didn't bring cookies.
clue: The mystery reader read 50 more pages than Ivan.
clue: Kofi isn't reading sci-fi.
answer: Hana, mystery, 100, grapes
answer: Ivan, poetry, 50, cookies
answer: June, sci-fi, 125, apples
answer: Kofi, biography, 75, popcorn
